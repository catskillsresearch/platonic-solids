# Provenance

This repository is the substantive development of a Lean 4 / Mathlib
classification of the Platonic solids and the regular convex 4-polytopes
(polychora). It is not a thin wrapper and not a reimplementation of an
independent formalization.

The informal account is `PlatonicSolids.md`. The sorry-free development is
`PlatonicSolids.lean`. Palomar Comparator metadata lives at the repository
root (`Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`, `comparator.json`,
`formalization.yaml`).

The Lake package, arXiv PDF pipeline, and Palomar packaging follow the
layout of
[`catskillsresearch/lrsodincic`](https://github.com/catskillsresearch/lrsodincic)
and
[`catskillsresearch/cardb`](https://github.com/catskillsresearch/cardb).
Those repositories are templates only; they contribute no mathematical
content.

Further work belongs in
[`catskillsresearch/platonic-solids`](https://github.com/catskillsresearch/platonic-solids).
Palomar registration is prepared here and is assigned a public registry ID
only after submission at
[submit.palomar-registry.org](https://submit.palomar-registry.org/).
